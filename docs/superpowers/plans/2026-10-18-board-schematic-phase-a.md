# Board Schematic, Phase A (everything the Oxford kit does not gate) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Regenerate the four schematic sheets for the E-marked-module board — feed A protection, a new feed B, the rails, the MCU/CAN sheet with its final pin map, and ten signal outputs on three quad smart high-side switches — verified by a netlist assertion script, leaving only the pod (aux) switch stage for when the Oxford kit arrives.

**Architecture:** The sheets stay text-generated by `hardware/scripts/gen_*.py` (never hand-edited). A new `check_netlist.py` encodes this plan's electrical intent as named check groups and is written first, failing against the plan3 netlist; each sheet task turns its group green. The chosen quad switch's pin numbers and application values live in one module, `quadpins.py`, which `symlib.py`, `gen_outputs.py` and `check_netlist.py` all import. **Layout is out of scope** — the pod switch's footprint is kit-gated, so `gen_pcb.py` is not run in this plan and `hardware/mccan.kicad_pcb` stays the abandoned plan3 board.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 generators emitting KiCad 10 s-expressions; `kicad-cli` 10.0 (ERC, netlist export); PlatformIO / Arduino-ESP32 for the one firmware task.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-emarked-module-redesign-design.md` (the `main` copy is current; Task 0 brings it to the PCB branch)

## Global Constraints

- Work on branch **`plan4-emarked-modules`** in a worktree at `D:/Projects/ESP32MCCAN-pcb` (spec §8: board work inherits `hardware/scripts/`). Only Task 10 touches `main`.
- Sheets are generated, **never hand-edited**. Every generator must stay idempotent: running it twice leaves the file byte-identical.
- Run order: `symlib.py` first, then `gen_power_input.py`, `gen_rails.py`, `gen_mcu_can.py`, `gen_outputs.py`, `gen_root.py`, all from `hardware/scripts/`.
- `kicad-cli` is **not on PATH**: `"/c/Program Files/KiCad/10.0/bin/kicad-cli.exe"`. ERC and netlist export can exceed 2 min — use a 180 s timeout.
- **Do not run `gen_pcb.py`**, any `layout_*`/`route_*` script, or DRC in this plan.
- **Never `git add -A`.** Datasheet PDFs and their `pdftotext` extractions are gitignored third-party content; extract to the scratchpad, stage files by name.
- Datasheets are read from `hardware/datasheets/` with `/mingw64/bin/pdftotext -layout`. Manufacturer sites block automated fetching: a missing datasheet is a request to the user, not a web fetch.
- Every part on `VBAT`, `VBAT_PROT` or `VBAT_B` must survive the **SMBJ24A's 50.6 V 8/20 µs clamp** (fpmap.py docstring) — capacitors there are **≥ 63 V**, 0805 or larger.
- Every enable net carries an external pull-down (hardware-review-lessons: missed three times on plan3).
- An alternative-build part is a **DNP flag**, never a sentence in sheet text.
- Feed A's protected net keeps its existing name **`VBAT`**; the new feed is **`VBAT_B`**. They are never joined by any net or part (spec §4.1.1).
- **No lamp output on a strapping pin** (GPIO 0, 2, 5, 12, 15), and none on GPIO 14, which toggles during boot.

## Review Focus

1. **Lamp lit at reset.** A GPIO floats or is pulled high while the ESP32 boots, and a switch input sees it → every switch `IN` net must have a resistor to `GND` and no path to a supply. Test: `boot_safety` group (Task 2).
2. **Feed B fitted backwards / the TVS on the wrong side.** A reverse-connected feed B must be blocked by the P-FET, and the unidirectional TVS must sit on the protected side or it shorts the reversed supply → FET drain on the connector, source on `VBAT_B`, TVS cathode on `VBAT_B`. Test: `feed_b` group (Task 2).
3. **The two feeds bridged.** Any part with one pin on `VBAT` and another on `VBAT_B` re-merges both pods through one 5 A fuse → no such part, no shared net. Test: `feed_b` group.
4. **A fault drives a sense pin above 3.3 V into the ADC.** Smart-switch sense outputs go to a fault level on short or over-temperature → every `SNS_x` net reaches the MCU through a series resistor and a Schottky clamp to `+3V3_ALW`. Test: `outputs` group.
5. **CAN never wakes or never receives.** The RX pin must be RTC-capable for EXT1 wake, and the transceiver's `+5V` must be under an enable that defaults off but that the firmware drives on → `CAN_RXD` on GPIO35, `EN_5V` pulled down, firmware drives `EN_5V` high and `CAN_STB` low. Test: `mcu` group (Task 2) and Task 10's build.

## Decisions this plan applies (made 2026-10-18, before Task 1)

These are fixed inputs. A task that finds one of them impossible stops and reports rather than choosing another.

**D1. Three quad switches, not two.** Spec §4.4 says "two quad-channel parts for the ten signal outputs", which is eight channels. User decision: **three quads, 12 channels, 2 spare.** Channel allocation, chosen so that one dead chip never takes out both sides' indicators, and brake survives a side chip failing:

| Chip | Ref | Ch1 | Ch2 | Ch3 | Ch4 | Sense net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (left) | U11 | `FL_DRL` | `FL_IND` | `RL_DRL` | `RL_IND` | `SNS_A` |
| B (right) | U12 | `FR_DRL` | `FR_IND` | `RR_DRL` | `RR_IND` | `SNS_B` |
| C (brake) | U13 | `RL_BRK` | `RR_BRK` | spare, reserved for `AUX_MODE` | spare | `SNS_C` |

**D2. Final ESP32 pin map.** Module pin numbers are ESP32-WROOM-32E pad numbers, as in the plan3 netlist.

| Net | GPIO | Pad | Why this pin |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OUT_FL_DRL` | 25 | 10 | non-strapping output |
| `OUT_FL_IND` | 26 | 11 | " |
| `OUT_FR_DRL` | 32 | 8 | " |
| `OUT_FR_IND` | 33 | 9 | " |
| `OUT_RL_DRL` | 27 | 12 | " |
| `OUT_RL_IND` | 16 | 27 | " (free on WROOM: no PSRAM) |
| `OUT_RL_BRK` | 17 | 28 | " |
| `OUT_RR_DRL` | 18 | 30 | " |
| `OUT_RR_IND` | 19 | 31 | " |
| `OUT_RR_BRK` | 21 | 33 | " (was I2C SDA) |
| `OUT_AUX_L` | 22 | 36 | " (was I2C SCL) — terminated in a pull-down until the kit plan |
| `OUT_AUX_R` | 23 | 37 | " — as above |
| `EN_5V` | 4 | 26 | enable, RTC-capable so it can be held in sleep |
| `LED_STAT` | 13 | 16 | unchanged from plan3 |
| `CAN_STB` | 14 | 13 | unchanged; a boot-time toggle on standby is harmless |
| `CAN_RXD` | 35 | 7 | input-only, **RTC GPIO → EXT1 wake** (firmware currently says 16, which cannot wake) |
| `DIAG_EN` | 2 | 24 | strapping, must be low for download — the pull-down serves both |
| `DIAG_SEL0` | 5 | 29 | strapping; a select line's boot-time level is harmless while `DIAG_EN` is low |
| `DIAG_SEL1` | 15 | 23 | strapping; as above |
| `SNS_A` | 34 | 6 | ADC1 (ADC2 is unusable with Wi-Fi up) |
| `SNS_B` | 36 / SENSOR_VP | 4 | ADC1; freed by deleting `IGN_SENSE` |
| `SNS_C` | 39 / SENSOR_VN | 5 | ADC1 |
| `IO12_STRAP` | 12 | 14 | keeps plan3's pull-down; also the firmware's TWAI TX pin (D4) |
| `BOOT_N`, `EN_MCU`, `UART_RX`, `UART_TX` | 0, EN, 3, 1 | 25, 3, 34, 35 | unchanged |

`DIAG_EN`, `DIAG_SEL0` and `DIAG_SEL1` are **shared by all three chips**; each chip has its own sense line. `DIAG_SEL0`/`DIAG_SEL1` get **no external pull**: their level is irrelevant while `DIAG_EN` is low, and a pull-down on GPIO15 would mute the boot ROM log.

**D3. Ignition sense is deleted.** Spec §6.2: CAN is off when the bike is off, so bus activity is the wake source. `IGN_IN` (J1 cavity 1), `IGN_SENSE` and their divider/clamp parts go; J1 becomes 2-pin.

**D4. The CAN TX GPIO link is deleted; listen-only stays hardware-enforced.** Plan3 joined GPIO17 to the TJA1042 `TXD` through DNP 0R `R23`. GPIO17 is now a lamp output, and the TWAI peripheral idles its TX pin **high** — so if the firmware's TX pin were a lamp pin, that lamp would light. `R23` is removed, `TXD` stays pulled up by `R24`, and the firmware points TWAI TX at **GPIO12**, which has no connection but its strap pull-down (it is sampled only at reset, long before TWAI starts).

**D5. Harness tails.** KiCad's `Connector_Wire` relief footprints stop at 1x06, so the rear 7-conductor run is two tails on one 7-core cable through one gland: one return, as spec §3.1 requires.

| Ref | Footprint | Pin 1..n |
|---|---|---|
| J1 (feed A) | `SolderWire-1.5sqmm_1x02_P6mm_D1.7mm_OD3mm_Relief` | `FEED_P`, `GND_IN` |
| J9 (feed B) | `SolderWire-1.5sqmm_1x02_P6mm_D1.7mm_OD3mm_Relief` | `FEED_B_P`, `GND_B` |
| J3 (front) | `SolderWire-0.5sqmm_1x05_P4.6mm_D0.9mm_OD2.1mm_Relief` | `FL_DRL`, `FL_IND`, `FR_DRL`, `FR_IND`, `GND` |
| J4 (rear, part 1) | `SolderWire-0.5sqmm_1x04_P4.6mm_D0.9mm_OD2.1mm_Relief` | `RL_DRL`, `RL_IND`, `RL_BRK`, `GND` |
| J5 (rear, part 2) | `SolderWire-0.5sqmm_1x03_P4.6mm_D0.9mm_OD2.1mm_Relief` | `RR_DRL`, `RR_IND`, `RR_BRK` |
| J8 (CAN) | unchanged | unchanged |

J6 and J7 (plan3's RR corner and Denali) are deleted. The pod tail is kit-gated and is not added here.

**D6. Feed B circuit** (spec §4.1.1: reverse polarity + 24 V TVS, no buck, no filter, no LTC4380). Reuses parts whose datasheets are already on disk.

| Ref | Part | Connection |
|---|---|---|
| Q13 | Vishay SQJ461EP, P-ch −60 V, 16 mΩ, VGS ±20 V (`sqj461ep.pdf`) | D → `FEED_B_P`, S → `VBAT_B`, G → `GATE_B` |
| R108 | 100 kΩ 0603 | `GATE_B` → `GND_B` |
| D24 | MMSZ5245B, 15 V zener, SOD-123 | cathode `VBAT_B`, anode `GATE_B` (holds VGS inside ±20 V at the 50.6 V clamp) |
| D25 | SMBJ24A, SMB | cathode `VBAT_B`, anode `GND_B` — on the **protected** side |
| C66 | 100 nF 100 V 0805 | `VBAT_B` → `GND_B` |
| NT1 | `Device:NetTie_2`, `NetTie:NetTie-2_SMD_Pad0.5mm` | `GND_B` ↔ `GND`: the star point, a deliberate object for the layout phase |

At 2.48 A worst case (spec §4.1.1, 10.5 V) Q13 dissipates 2.48² × 16 mΩ ≈ 0.10 W. `VBAT_B` ends at a root-level label for the kit plan's `AUX_R` switch.

**D7. LTC4380 current limit — recorded, not changed.** `R100` = 5.6 mΩ sets 50 mV / 5.6 mΩ ≈ **9 A** (`LTC4380.pdf`, VSNS 45–55 mV), behind feed A's **4 A** fuse, so it can never act. Re-sizing it needs pod L's start-up current on feed A, which is spec §9.1 item 2: **kit-gated**. `R100` is left as is, and Task 9 records the gap in spec §9.1.

### Task 2: `check_netlist.py` — the plan's tests, written first

**Files:**
- Create: `hardware/scripts/check_netlist.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `quadpins.PINS`, D1–D6 above
- Produces:
  - `load(path: str) -> Netlist`, where `Netlist.nets: dict[str, set[str]]` maps a net name with its sheet prefix stripped (`/mcu_can/CAN_RXD` → `CAN_RXD`) to `{"U5.7", ...}`, and `Netlist.parts: dict[str, Part]`, `Part(value: str, footprint: str, dnp: bool)`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the checks**

Values come from D1–D6 and `quadpins`; each group is a list of small functions. Required assertions:

- `deleted`: no part whose value is `LM51571-Q1`, `DML3017LDC`, `PCA9685PW/Q900`, `ADG706`, `NX5020UNBKS`, `BTS7008-2EPA` or `1206L050/24`; no net named `+24V`, `+3V3_SW`, `EN_BOOST`, `EN_3V3SW`, `EN_DIAG`, `IGN_IN`, `IGN_SENSE`, `I2C_SDA`, `I2C_SCL`, `MUX_S0`..`MUX_S3`, `RGB_ISNS`, `ISNS_DEN`, `PWM_DEN_A`, `PWM_DEN_B`, `DEN_DSEL`; no ref `R23`, `J6`, `J7`.
- `feed_b`: D6's table, pin by pin (Q13 pin roles taken from the symbol's G/S/D map); `VBAT` and `VBAT_B` are different nets; **no part has one pin on `VBAT` and another on `VBAT_B`**; `GND_B` contains only J9.2, R108, D25, C66 and NT1.
- `rails`: `EN_5V` contains U5.26, the 5 V buck's `EN/UVLO` pin and a resistor whose other pin is on `GND`; `+5V` still reaches U6 `VCC`.
- `mcu`: every row of D2 — `U5.<pad>` is on the named net; `CAN_RXD` is on U5.7; `IO12_STRAP` has a resistor to `GND`; `U6.1` (`TXD`) is on a net with no U5 pin and a resistor to `+3V3_ALW` (R24 — the hardware listen-only guarantee); `CAN_STB` has a resistor to `+3V3_ALW` (R25, standby by default).
- `outputs`: for each of the 12 `(chip, channel)` cells of D1 that carries a function: `OUT_<fn>` contains U5's pad, the series resistor, and nothing else on the MCU side; the chip's `IN<ch>` pin is on a net with the series resistor's other pin and an `R_IN_PULLDOWN` resistor to `GND`; the chip's `OUT<ch>` pads are all on net `<fn>`. Spare channels: `IN` pulled down, `OUT` unconnected. `OUT_AUX_L`/`OUT_AUX_R`: U5's pad plus a pull-down to `GND`, nothing else. Each chip's `SNS` pin sits on a net with `R_SNS` to `GND` and a series resistor whose other end is on `SNS_A|B|C`, and that net also has a Schottky whose cathode is on `+3V3_ALW`. Each chip's `VS` pins are on `VBAT`, `GND` pins on `GND`.
- `harness`: D5's table, pin by pin.
- `boot_safety`: every net containing any chip's `IN*` pin has at least one resistor to `GND` and no part pin on `+3V3_ALW`, `+5V`, `VBAT` or `VBAT_B`; no `OUT_*` net contains U5 pads 13, 14, 23, 24, 25 or 29 (GPIO 14, 12, 15, 2, 0, 5).
